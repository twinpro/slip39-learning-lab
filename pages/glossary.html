Glossary

Plain-language definitions.

BIP

Bitcoin Improvement Proposal.

BIP-39

Mnemonic code for generating deterministic wallet seeds.

SLIP

SatoshiLabs Improvement Proposal.

SLIP-39

Mnemonic threshold secret-sharing standard.

SSS

Shamir’s Secret Sharing.

Share

One fragment produced by a secret-sharing system.

Threshold

Minimum number of shares or groups required.

Mnemonic

Human-readable words used to represent encoded data.

Entropy

Randomness used as source material for cryptographic secrets.

Multisig

Wallet policy requiring multiple signatures.

Educational only. No wallet generation, cryptography, or storage of secrets.
