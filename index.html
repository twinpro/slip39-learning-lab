1 secret

SLIP-39 starts with one recovery secret and splits it into multiple human-readable shares.

Threshold

A rule such as 3-of-5 means any 3 valid shares can recover the secret. Two cannot.

Groups

You can require enough people inside enough groups. This is useful for families, companies and committees.

How recovery works

1. Create shares.
A wallet generates SLIP-39 recovery shares.
2. Distribute shares.
Each holder stores one share independently.
3. Meet thresholds.
Enough people and groups cooperate.
4. Recover the wallet.
The underlying secret is reconstructed on a trusted recovery device.

What to test before real deployment

  • What happens when members leave the LLC?
  • How are shares replaced or rotated?
  • Who supervises recovery?
  • Where is recovery performed?
  • What prevents one recovered wallet from becoming a single point of control?

SLIP-39 versus Bitcoin multisig

QuestionSLIP-39Multisig
What is distributed?Shares of one recovery secretIndependent signing keys
Required for each spend?Usually noYes
Main purposeBackup and recoveryOngoing transaction control
Single-point riskReconstructed secret can become oneReduced if keys remain separate
Operational complexityModerateHigher

Practical takeaway

SLIP-39 can be a strong recovery layer. It is not automatically a replacement for institutional multisignature custody.
