SLIP-39 Recovery Learning Sheet

1. Full names

TermFull namePurpose
BIP-39Bitcoin Improvement Proposal 39Mnemonic wallet backup standard
SLIP-39SatoshiLabs Improvement Proposal 39Mnemonic threshold recovery shares
SSSShamir’s Secret SharingSplit a secret into threshold shares
MultisigMultiple signaturesRequire independent keys to authorize spending

2. The essential distinction

BIP-39: typically one complete mnemonic backup.

SLIP-39: multiple mnemonic recovery shares with a threshold.

Multisig: multiple independent keys required for transactions.

3. Example structure

Twenty-one participants can be modeled as seven groups of three. A hypothetical rule could require two of three participants in each qualifying group and five of seven qualifying groups.

Minimum correctly distributed cooperation: 5 × 2 = 10 participants.

4. Warnings

  • Never manually split a BIP-39 phrase by distributing individual words.
  • Never enter real recovery shares into this educational website.
  • Green “recovery available” results measure availability, not complete security.
  • Verify wallet compatibility and perform a test recovery before funding.
  • Use legal, tax, and professional custody review for an group.
