STANDARD

BIP-39

Bitcoin Improvement Proposal 39 defines mnemonic words used to represent wallet entropy and derive deterministic wallet seeds.

Common form

Users commonly encounter 12-word or 24-word mnemonic phrases.

Strength

Broad wallet support and a simple backup model.

Weakness

A full mnemonic is typically a single point of failure.
