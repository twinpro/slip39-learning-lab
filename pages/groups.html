ADVANCED RECOVERY

Grouped Recovery

SLIP-39 can combine member thresholds inside groups with a group threshold across the group.

Example

Seven groups contain three members each. A group qualifies with any two members. Recovery requires five qualifying groups.

2 of 3

2 of 3

2 of 3

Recovery
Threshold design is a tradeoff. Too low increases collusion risk. Too high increases lockout risk.
