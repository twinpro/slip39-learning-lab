Shamir’s Secret Sharing

SSS means Shamir’s Secret Sharing, named after cryptographer Adi Shamir.

Core idea

Split one secret into multiple shares. Choose a threshold. Fewer than the threshold cannot reconstruct the secret; enough valid shares can.
