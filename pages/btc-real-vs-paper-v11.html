What V11 can and cannot prove

Facts: ETF flows come from SoSoValue's official API. Coinbase and Kraken public USD spot prices are averaged and compared with OKX BTC-USDT. Public derivatives data is aggregated from every working free venue in the snapshot, including OKX, Deribit, BitMEX, Hyperliquid, and Kraken Futures when reachable.

Limitation: exchange-balance data requires continuously maintained address labels identifying which BTC addresses belong to exchanges. No trustworthy free machine-readable all-exchange balance feed has been verified for this automated build. Exchange supply therefore stays ⚪ UNKNOWN and is excluded rather than guessed.

Interpretation: high open interest is not automatically bearish and is not proof of manipulation. V11 treats derivatives as a leverage/crowding signal, not as evidence of manipulation.
