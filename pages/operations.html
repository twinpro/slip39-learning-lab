GOVERNANCE

Operational Planning

A strong cryptographic design can still fail through weak procedures.

Share inventory

Record who received a share without recording the share words.

Departures

Define what happens when a member leaves, dies, becomes incapacitated, or refuses cooperation.

Recovery ceremony

Define who convenes recovery, where it occurs, which devices are allowed, and who witnesses it.

Post-recovery action

Move funds or rotate recovery material if the old structure is no longer trustworthy.

For an group, the custody procedure should align with the operating agreement, formal resolutions, tax records, insurance requirements, and legal advice.
