What is a SLIP?

SLIP means SatoshiLabs Improvement Proposal.

SLIPs are technical standards published by SatoshiLabs. They are separate from Bitcoin Improvement Proposals, even when they address wallet interoperability.

SLIP-10

Private key derivation across supported elliptic curves.

SLIP-39

Mnemonic secret sharing for wallet recovery.

SLIP-44

Registered coin type identifiers.
