Facts vs interpretation

kept separate on purpose
Facts: ETF flows, exchange BTC balances, Coinbase-vs-offshore spot premium, futures open interest and funding are measured inputs.
Interpretation: V7 maps those inputs to green / yellow / red with transparent thresholds in this page. The thresholds are judgment calls, not laws of finance.
Not claimed: High futures activity is not automatically bearish and is not proof of manipulation. Futures have both long and short sides, and institutions can hedge derivatives with actual BTC.
