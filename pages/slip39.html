STANDARD

SLIP-39

SatoshiLabs Improvement Proposal 39, formally titled Shamir’s Secret-Sharing for Mnemonic Codes defines mnemonic recovery shares using threshold secret sharing.

Share threshold

Example: 3-of-5 means any three valid shares can meet the recovery threshold.

Group threshold

Groups can each have an internal threshold, plus an overall threshold across groups.

Resilience

A lost share need not destroy recovery, and one stolen share need not reveal the secret.
